RAGite 1.0 is out. You can find it here: [pihme.github.io/ragite](https://pihme.github.io/ragite/) (source on [GitHub](https://github.com/pihme/ragite)).

# What is RAGite?
RAGite is 80% of what you need for RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation). It combines a chunker with an embedding model
that runs on your CPU. The chunks and their embeddings are then stored in an [SQLite](https://sqlite.org) virtual table from which you can 
query them.

# Who is it for?
At this point it is mostly for developers who want to understand and experiment with RAG and as a basis to tinker 
around with it. It comes with some defaults, and you can adapt it to what you need it to be. Either fork it, change 
the exhaustive spec and have your agents make the changes for you. Or look at [`tinkering.md`](https://github.com/pihme/ragite/blob/main/docs/tinkering.md) in the repo for ideas where 
to start yourself.
